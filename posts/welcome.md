This is where I keep the interactive simulations I use in class, along with the
occasional note on the ideas behind them.

The goal is simple: build intuition you can *see* and *touch*. Every simulation
runs live in your browser, so you can drag a point, tune a slider, and watch the
mathematics respond in real time. Change a learning rate until gradient descent
diverges, shrink a step until a secant becomes a tangent, or add harmonics until
a sum of sines snaps into a square wave.

The [simulations](/simulations) are grouped by area: calculus, linear algebra,
statistics, machine learning, deep learning, generative AI and physics. Pick a
topic and start poking at it.

A few things worth knowing:

- Every simulation has a **What to observe** section with a handful of guided
  questions, in case you want a nudge toward the interesting behaviour.
- Keyboard shortcuts work everywhere: <kbd>space</kbd> to run or pause,
  <kbd>s</kbd> to step, <kbd>r</kbd> to reset, <kbd>f</kbd> for fullscreen.
- There is a light and a dark theme (the toggle sits in the top-right), and a
  **Link** button that copies a shareable URL of your current setup.

Everything here is open on [GitHub](https://github.com/silvaan). If a simulation
would help make an idea click, feel free to reach out.
